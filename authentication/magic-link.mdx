## What is Magic Link?

Magic link is a passwordless authentication method. Instead of remembering a password, you click a link sent to your email.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Your Email">
    On the login page, type your email address and click **Continue**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Your Inbox">
    You'll receive an email from Granite with a magic link

    ```
    Subject: Sign in to Granite

    Click this link to sign in:
    [Sign in to Granite →]

    This link expires in 1 hour.
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the Link">
    Clicking the link automatically signs you in and redirects to the dashboard
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security Considerations

| Aspect | How It's Handled |
|--------|------------------|
| **Link expiration** | 1 hour validity |
| **Single use** | Each link works once |
| **Email security** | Relies on your email account security |
| **No stored secrets** | Granite never stores your password |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't receive the email">
    - Check spam/junk folder
    - Verify you entered the correct email
    - Wait a minute and try again
    - Contact your IT if your company blocks emails
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Link says 'expired' or 'invalid'">
    - Links expire after 1 hour
    - Each link works only once
    - Request a new link from the login page
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Link opens wrong page">
    - Clear your browser cache
    - Try a different browser
    - Make sure you're clicking the full link
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
